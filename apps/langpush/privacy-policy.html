Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kaindra Rizq Sachio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and retains information when you use the Langpush - AI Language Tutor mobile application (the "Application").

The categories of information and the ways we process it may depend on how you use the Application, the features you choose, and the app build available to you.

We may collect information directly from you, automatically from your use of the Application and device, and from service providers when you choose sign-in, subscription, support, or other connected features.

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with the Application, the features you use, and the settings available in your app build.

Information You Provide or Create

  • Account details, such as an email address, name, profile image, and account identifier
  • Learning information, such as languages, goals, interests, proficiency, preferences, and progress
  • Conversation information, such as messages, conversation history, transcripts, feedback, corrections, vocabulary, and grammar activity
  • Support information that you choose to send when contacting us through in-app support chat
  • Subscription and purchase information needed to provide paid features and restore access

If you use a third-party sign-in option, we may receive the account information you authorize through that provider, subject to its settings and policies.

Voice and Speech Features

When you choose to use voice features, the Application may process microphone audio, transcripts, and speech or pronunciation feedback. Voice and AI features may use third-party AI, speech, translation, or pronunciation service providers. The providers used may vary depending on the feature, platform, region, or app build.

Transcripts may be stored with the related conversation. Short audio clips may be stored temporarily on your device for replay. We do not use voice features to display advertising. Please do not use voice features to share sensitive information that you do not want processed by these providers.

Information Collected When You Use the Application

  • Usage, progress, and feature-performance information
  • Technical and diagnostic information used for security, analytics, and error reporting
  • Subscription, purchase, restore, and entitlement status
  • Attribution and app activity information in builds where attribution tools are enabled

Some builds may use product analytics or limited replay with masking controls. These features may be disabled or configured differently depending on the build you use.

Information Stored on Your Device

The Application may store session information, preferences, learning settings, grammar drafts, and temporary audio replay files on your device. These files may be removed by the Application, your device, or when the Application is uninstalled.

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • Precise location data
  • Contacts or phonebook data
  • Camera data for Application features
  • Third-party advertisements displayed inside the Application

How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • To create, secure, and maintain your account
  • To provide language-learning, conversation, translation, feedback, voice, and pronunciation features
  • To save and display your conversations, learning activity, vocabulary, corrections, and progress
  • To provide subscriptions, restore purchases, and manage access to paid features
  • To understand usage, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, and improve the Application
  • To respond to support requests and service-related communications
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect the Application, users, and service integrity

We may share information with service providers that help us provide, secure, analyze, and support the Application. These providers may process information on our behalf and only as needed for the services they provide. We require service providers to protect information in a manner consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable law. The categories of providers may include:

  • Account, hosting, storage, and application infrastructure providers

Advertising and Attribution

The Application does not display third-party advertisements. In builds where attribution is enabled, AppsFlyer may process device and technical information, app activity, and completed subscription conversion events to measure advertising effectiveness. The Application does not send chat messages or transcripts through AppsFlyer.

On iOS, the Application may request App Tracking Transparency permission during onboarding or after sign-in. AppsFlyer receives the IDFA only if you grant that system permission. You can change this choice in your iOS privacy settings. Declining permission does not prevent you from using the Application, although advertising measurement may be less precise.

Data Retention

We retain account records, conversations, learning activity, and related information for as long as reasonably needed to provide and improve the Application, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Third-party service providers may retain data they process under their own privacy notices and our applicable agreements with them. Their processing and retention may occur outside your country of residence.

Data stored locally on your device, such as preferences and grammar drafts, remains on your device until it is removed by the Application, overwritten, or cleared through app removal or device/app storage controls.

Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Application account and associated first-party app data directly in the Application:

  1. Open Profile.
  2. In the Account section, select Delete Account.
  3. Review the confirmation message and tap Delete account.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. The Application sends the request to our server, which removes the account and associated first-party data linked to it. We aim to remove the Application data associated with the account, subject to information we may need to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or compliance reasons. Data stored locally on your device may remain until you remove the Application or clear its storage.

Deleting your Application account does not necessarily remove information already processed by independent third-party providers, such as support-chat, attribution, subscription, or diagnostic providers. We handle such requests according to the provider's policies, our applicable agreements, and the law. If you need help with account or data deletion, contact us at kaindradev@gmail.com.
